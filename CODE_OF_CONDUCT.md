# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as addresses, phone numbers or
  order details, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Responsibilities and Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do
not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour
is harmful.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **orders@koreanhive.com**. All reports will
be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including this repository,
its issues and pull requests, and when someone officially represents the
project in public.
